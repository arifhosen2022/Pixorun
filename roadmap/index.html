PIXORUN ROADMAP

Development Journey

Building the PIXORUN ecosystem step by step, with the community at its foundation.

Build the Foundation

Genesis Pass

Introduce the first PIXORUN NFT collection and build the early community around the ecosystem.

Private Beta Testing

Begin controlled testing of selected ecosystem components, gather feedback and improve the experience.

04 / PUBLIC BETA

Public Beta Testnet

Expand testing, invite broader community participation and refine products based on real feedback.

Ecosystem Expansion & Mainnet Preparation

Develop and expand ecosystem products—including the future reputation-focused launchpad—and prepare for mainnet when the foundation is ready.
