Quality Contributions

Genuine Referrals

Referral points are credited only after the referred participant passes verification and completes qualifying activity.

Points & GTD

Points are not a token allocation promise. GTD eligibility, if offered, will be determined under published criteria and verification rules. High points do not automatically guarantee GTD.

Spam, fake accounts, duplicate wallets, automated activity and Sybil behaviour may lead to point removal or disqualification.

Referral

Your personal referral link will be available after profile creation. Only genuine referrals who complete required activity can qualify for points.
