Official information and participation rules.

Participation Rules

One genuine participant, one primary profile and one verified EVM wallet. No minimum X follower requirement.

Points Policy

Points are awarded only for eligible missions after verification. Low-effort spam, duplicate submissions and automated activity are not eligible.

Referral Policy

Referral points require genuine new participants who complete qualifying activity. Self-referrals, duplicate accounts and referral abuse may be disqualified.

GTD Eligibility

Points do not guarantee GTD or token allocation. Any GTD decision will follow separately published eligibility criteria and verification.

Privacy & Data

Account connection and wallet information should be used only for profile, security and eligibility verification. Full privacy terms will be published before account features launch.

Risk Disclaimer

Participation does not guarantee rewards, token value, listing, allocation or financial return. Web3 activity involves risk.
